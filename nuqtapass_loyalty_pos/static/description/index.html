NuqtaPass Loyalty – Point of Sale

The NuqtaPass button on your Odoo till: stamps, points, credit and rewards for every paid order.

Point of SaleWallet card scanEnglish + العربية

Earns at Validate

Paid till orders give the stamp or points. Refunds take them back, with the credit back on its own line.

Find the customer fast

Type the phone or scan their Wallet card with a 2D (QR) scanner.

Spend at the till

Points as money, NuqtaPass credit next to Cash and Card, pack visits, rewards and vouchers.

New customer? Show the QR

They scan it and add their card to Apple Wallet or Google Wallet.

Installs by itself with NuqtaPass Loyalty. What is sent and the Business plan it needs are described in NuqtaPass Loyalty.
